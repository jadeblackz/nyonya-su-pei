Private dining

A Nyonya dinner, cooked just for your table

Intimate private dining in George Town. Traditional family recipes, served on Nyonya porcelain, at a pace that lets conversation breathe.

Enquire for a date

How private dining works

Tell us the number of guests, any diets, and the kind of evening you want. Nyonya Su Pei proposes a menu and a time. You sit down. She cooks.

Family table

A birthday, a reunion, or a quiet Sunday. The menu can follow old festival dishes or stay simple.

Guests from afar

A private introduction to Penang Nyonya cooking — without a crowded restaurant.

A slow evening

Courses come when they are ready. Stories of the recipes sit between plates.

The menu

Built around the market that morning

The menu is curated from what is fresh at the market that morning. Expect the four notes of Nyonya cooking — spicy, sour, sweet, and the deep savour of belacan. Vegetarian plates are cooked with the same care.

Dining is at 53 Jalan Padang Victoria, George Town, Penang 10400, with Heritage Artisans — or at a table we arrange with you.

Private dining is RM350 nett per person for a 7-course experience. Send a request and we will reply with availability.
