About

A Nyonya kitchen, open to guests

Nyonya Su Pei cooks and teaches the Peranakan food of Penang — family recipes, market knowledge, and the unhurried craft of rempah.

Award-winning chef, heritage teacher

Su Pei holds a Professional Diploma in Malaysian Heritage Culinary and has more than ten years at the stove as a teacher and private dining chef. Guests know her for clear explanations, a tidy kitchen, and a gift for making traditional work feel possible.

She teaches in George Town, hosts private tables, and travels for guest classes in Singapore and the Klang Valley. Alongside the food, she talks about Baba Nyonya history — why a sour laksa belongs to Penang, why keluak is soaked for days, why a sambal must be fried until the oil rises.

The school sits at 1-Stop Midlands Park Center on Jalan Burma. Private dining is nearby at 53 Jalan Padang Victoria. Both are small on purpose.

Nyonya Su Pei is a brand of The Heritage Artisans. Classes and private dining are operated by The Heritage Artisans.

A kitchen that keeps the old ways useful

From scratch

No packets. Spice pastes are pounded, stocks are built, and kuih are folded by hand — so you can repeat the work at home.

In English

Teaching is clear and unhurried. Recipes go home printed in English. Questions are part of the class, not an interruption.

For every table

Solo travellers, families, and vegetarian guests are welcome. Tell us what you need; the menu can bend without losing its character.

Come cook, or come eat

Book a class, a private dinner, or leave your name for recipes and videos. WhatsApp is often the fastest way to reach us.
