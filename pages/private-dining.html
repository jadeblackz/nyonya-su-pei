Private dining

A calm, home-style Nyonya meal prepared for your table. Tell us the occasion and guest count — we’ll suggest a clear set menu.

How it works

Three simple steps, no confusing packages.

  • Send the date, time, and number of guests
  • We propose a set menu and a clear price
  • You confirm, and we cook and serve

Sample starting point for this mock-up: from RM 120 per person (minimums may apply).

Popular set themes

  • Family reunion table
  • Festive Nyonya feast
  • Quiet celebration dinner
  • Guest welcome meal

What guests usually enjoy

Shared starters

Otak-otak, acar, and light kueh to open the meal without rushing.

Main dishes

A balanced spread of meat, seafood, and vegetable Nyonya classics.

Sweet finish

Traditional desserts or a simple fruit course — your choice.
