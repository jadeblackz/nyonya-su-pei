Cooking classes

Learn Nyonya cooking in a small kitchen

A half-day class in George Town with award-winning chef Nyonya Su Pei. We start at the market, cook from scratch, and sit down to lunch.

Book a class

What the morning looks like

Classes usually run from about 9 in the morning until 2 in the afternoon. Seats are limited — often fewer than eight — so everyone has room at the counter.

  1. 1

    Meet at the market

    We begin at Chowrasta Market. Su Pei shows you the herbs, fish, and spices you will cook with — and why they matter.

  2. 2

    Cook together, slowly

    In a clean, small kitchen you grind rempah, wrap, stew, and fry. Everything is from scratch. Questions are welcome.

  3. 3

    Sit down to lunch

    You eat what you made. Printed English recipes come home with you, so the dishes can live in your kitchen too.

Included

Everything you need for the day

  • Guided walk through Chowrasta Market
  • Hands-on cooking of three or four dishes
  • Rempah and techniques explained slowly
  • Lunch of the food you cooked
  • Printed recipes in English
  • Vegetarian and allergy-friendly menus on request

Where we meet

The cooking school is at 1-Stop Midlands Park Center on Jalan Burma, George Town. Su Pei will send a map pin before class, including the market meeting point.

Penang

488D-04-07, 1-Stop Midlands Park Center, Jalan Burma. George Town, Penang 10350. Home kitchen classes and private client meals are also arranged by request.

Singapore and Kuala Lumpur

Su Pei also teaches guest classes — including at The Kitchen Society in Singapore and sessions in Petaling Jaya. Ask when the next date is open.
